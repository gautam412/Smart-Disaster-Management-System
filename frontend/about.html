About the Smart Disaster Management System

The Smart Disaster Management System for Uttarakhand is designed to provide real-time disaster alerts, resources, and support to residents and visitors in the state. Our platform integrates advanced technology to ensure timely information dissemination and effective response to natural disasters such as floods, landslides, and cloudbursts.

Our Mission

To save lives and minimize damage by providing accurate, timely, and accessible disaster management information to the people of Uttarakhand.

Key Features

  • Real-time disaster alerts and warnings
  • Interactive maps showing risk areas
  • Incident reporting system
  • Emergency helplines and resources
  • Disaster preparedness guides and checklists
  • Photo gallery for awareness
