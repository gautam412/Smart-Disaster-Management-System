Current Disaster Alerts

⚠️ Flash Flood Warning - Uttarkashi

Date: September 2025

Description: Heavy rainfall expected in the region. Rivers may overflow. Evacuate low-lying areas immediately.

Advice: Move to higher ground, avoid crossing flooded areas, stay tuned for updates.

⚠️ Landslide Risk - Gangotri Highway

Date: Ongoing

Description: Increased landslide activity due to recent seismic activity. Road closures possible.

Advice: Avoid travel on affected highways, follow detour signs, monitor local news.

⚠️ Cloudburst Alert - Chamoli District

Date: August 2025

Description: Sudden heavy rain leading to flash floods in mountainous areas.

Advice: Seek shelter indoors, avoid outdoor activities, prepare emergency kits.

How to Respond to Alerts

  • Stay informed through official channels.
  • Follow evacuation orders promptly.
  • Prepare an emergency kit with essentials.
  • Contact helplines: 108 (Medical), 112 (Emergency), 1070 (State Control Room).
