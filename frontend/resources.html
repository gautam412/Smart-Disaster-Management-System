Disaster Preparedness & Safety Tips

🌧️ Flood Safety

Move to higher ground immediately. Avoid walking or driving through floodwaters. Never underestimate the power of moving water.

🏔️ Landslide Prevention

Stay alert near hilly terrain. Evacuate if signs of movement appear. Avoid building or living near steep slopes.

☁️ Cloudburst Response

Seek shelter immediately. Avoid low-lying areas prone to flash floods. Stay indoors until the rain stops.

❄️ Avalanche Awareness

In snowy regions, avoid steep slopes. Carry avalanche beacons if venturing out. Travel with experienced guides.

🌍 Earthquake Readiness

Drop, Cover, and Hold On. Stay indoors until shaking stops. Secure heavy furniture and objects.

🎒 Emergency Kit Essentials

Keep water, torch, medicines, non-perishable food, first aid kit, and important documents ready.

📞 Emergency Contacts

Call 108 for medical emergencies, 112 for general assistance, 1070 for state control room.

🚨 Community Preparedness

Know your area's disaster risks. Participate in drills and community training. Help neighbors in need.

🏕️ Trekking Safety

Inform someone about your plans. Carry GPS device. Check weather forecasts. Avoid trekking alone.

🚗 Vehicle Safety

Carry extra fuel, water, and blankets. Avoid driving during heavy rain or landslides. Use chains in snow.

🏠 Home Preparation

Reinforce your home against disasters. Keep emergency supplies for at least 72 hours. Have a family emergency plan.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Communication

Designate a meeting point. Teach children emergency procedures. Keep a list of important contacts.
